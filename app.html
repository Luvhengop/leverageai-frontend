🔗 Context & Role Prompting Step 2 of 5
⏱ ~4 min left

One of the most powerful yet overlooked techniques is telling Claude who it is before asking it anything. Claude responds differently depending on the role and context you give it. A generic prompt gets a generic answer — a contextualised prompt gets expert-level output.

📋 Business Scenario
You're a marketing manager at a Cape Town-based fintech startup. You need to write a cold email to a potential enterprise client (a large retail bank) introducing your payment solution. You have 10 minutes.
💡 Technique: Start your prompt with "You are a [role] with [experience/context]…" before giving your actual task. Watch how the output changes compared to just asking directly.
